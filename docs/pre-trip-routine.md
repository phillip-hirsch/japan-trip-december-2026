# Pre-trip routine

Do this a day or two before December 6, so the Home Screen app is already logged in when you land. A login lasts as long as the Access session, one month ([Deploy](../README.md#deploy)), so it covers the whole Trip.

1. **Log in to the app in Safari on the iPhone.** Open https://japan-trip-december-2026.phillip-b52.workers.dev and log in with the one-time PIN sent to phillip@350home.com.
2. **Add it to the Home Screen** from Safari's Share menu, straight after logging in. iOS copies Safari's login into the Home Screen app once, when it is added. After that the two keep separate logins, so a later Safari login doesn't reach the app. If the app is already on the Home Screen, delete it first and add it again.
3. **Log in on the desktop**, in the browser you'll use to plan.

Open the Home Screen app once to check it opens without asking you to log in.

## If a login expires during the Trip

The app shows "login expired" and takes you to the Cloudflare Access login. Log in inside the Home Screen app, not in Safari. Anything you were typing comes back in its field, marked "not saved": tap Retry to save it. The PIN goes to the same address, so keep that mailbox reachable on the phone.
